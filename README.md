### 👨🏾‍💻 About Me

I'm a Full Stack Developer based in Uganda, building web and mobile products that solve real problems for real people — not just for demos.

Over the past 6+ years I've shipped production apps across fintech, civic tech, property management, enterprise SaaS, and field operations. Some of what I've built is currently used by 500+ people daily across Uganda, Rwanda, DRC, and Tanzania.

- 🌍 Based in **Mbarara, Uganda** — working with global teams remotely
- 🏘️ Built and own **NyumbanApp** — a full rental platform live on web, App Store & Play Store
- ⚡ Shipped **The ClarityDesk** — an offline-first civic app for South Sudan communities, from zero to both stores in under 3 months
- 🌱 At **Raising The Village**, built the implementation system for 500+ field workers across 4 countries and shipped an AI assistant adopted by 100% of program managers in 2 weeks
- 💬 Ask me about **React architecture, state management, offline-first mobile apps, and payment integrations**
- 📫 Reach me at **kinyeramo@gmail.com**
- 🌐 Portfolio: [kinyera-amos-portfolio.netlify.app](https://kinyera-amos-portfolio.netlify.app)
